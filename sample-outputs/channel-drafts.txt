Urban Futures: October workshop findings

The fictional Urban Futures project completed its first round of community mapping workshops in October 2026. Across three workshops, 48 residents shared how they use public spaces and where they encounter barriers to access.

Participants identified a need for more shaded seating, accessible footpaths, and welcoming spaces for intergenerational activities. These are preliminary qualitative findings from the workshop participants, not a representative survey of the city.

The research team will now organize the anonymized workshop notes into themes and invite participants to review the draft findings. A follow-up session is planned for November 2026. No policy commitments or measured improvements have been established at this stage.

Review the draft findings at the November follow-up session.
