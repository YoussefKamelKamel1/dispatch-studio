Urban Futures: October workshop findings

Published 2026-10-23 · Dispatch research team

The fictional Urban Futures project completed its first round of community mapping workshops in October 2026. Across three workshops, 48 residents shared how they use public spaces and where they encounter barriers to access. Participants identified a need for more shaded seating, accessible footpaths, and welcoming spaces for intergenerational activities.
